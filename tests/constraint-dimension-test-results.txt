DIV-05 closure:
- Draft 2020-12 integer-valued JSON numbers such as 1.0 and -2.0 are accepted by the structural schema.
- The semantic helper now normalizes those integral numeric representations to canonical integer values.
- Genuine non-integer numbers (for example 1.5 and -0.5) and booleans remain invalid.

Validation boundary:
- Authoring may omit recognized zero axes; normalization expands all seven axes.
- Explicit vector: {} means DimensionOne; absence of vector is invalid/missing, not dimensionless.
- v0.1 exponent domain is integer-valued by ADR-0020.
- Unit/backend metadata is not part of the normalized Dimension semantic payload.
